What is Voice-IQ?

Voice-IQ is HealthOS’s AI voice receptionist and call centre workspace for hospitals. It connects patient calls with hospital information, doctor availability, OPD appointment booking, callback tickets and call-quality review.

Can it book OPD appointments using doctor availability?

Yes. The booking workflow identifies the doctor or speciality, checks available slots for the requested date, collects patient details and confirms the selected appointment. Doctor schedules and leave exceptions are managed in the workspace.

Does it support Hindi and English conversations?

Voice-IQ supports Hindi-English patient conversations. Language behaviour, doctor names and hospital terminology should be checked during deployment for your hospital’s patient population.

What happens when an enquiry cannot be resolved?

The request can become a callback ticket for the hospital team. Staff can record callback attempts, schedule the next callback, add internal notes and record a resolution. Cancellation and reschedule requests can also be handed to staff for follow-up.

How is AI call quality evaluated?

The call-quality rubric evaluates speech-to-text, intent understanding, accuracy, prompt adherence, conversation flow and task completion on a 0–4 scale. Reviewers can inspect score reasons, issues, evidence and recommendations alongside recordings and transcripts.

Does it replace our HIS or clinical team?

Voice-IQ handles patient-access and call centre workflows. It does not replace clinical judgement or an entire hospital information system. Any connection to an existing HIS should be scoped and validated for the hospital before rollout.

How are urgent or emergency calls handled?

Voice-IQ includes an emergency escalation workflow. Routing destinations, staff coverage and escalation rules need to be configured for the hospital. The AI receptionist is not a clinical diagnosis service or a replacement for emergency services.
